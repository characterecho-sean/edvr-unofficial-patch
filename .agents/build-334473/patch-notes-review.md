# Patch notes N1-N9 vs EDVR, build 334473 (4.4.2.0) vs 332841 - 2026-10-20

Read-only review. Exe evidence: `analysis/EliteDangerous64.exe` vs `-334473.exe`. Data evidence: `Win64/EffectsBinary/*.arc` (new), `edvr_logs/shaders/*.dxbc` (167 old dumps), old flight logs.

## Method and headline

- `build_diff.py map` did not exist, so a throwaway mapper was used: functions from `.pdata` (chained entries merged), best shift search (0..+0x3400), then `objdump -d` of both exes with rel32, RIP and image-RVA displacements normalised (struct offsets, immediates and branch shapes kept), plus callee-prologue, .rdata-content and xref-count checks. Independent raw-text objdump diffs of 0x58B150, 0x4F4E10 and 0x4312040 agree (744/35/390 insns, 24/3/5 relocated-operand rows, 0 text diffs).
- **87 functions holding 159 bound RVAs: 0 CHANGED** (9 byte-identical, 78 RELOC-only), plus 19 pdata-less leaves/thunks (IDENT or RELOC). Walking direct callees from those 87 roots (1,448 functions, depth 7): 537 IDENT, 886 RELOC, 24 RELOC with in-code jump tables, **1 CHANGED** (below). All 39 hook prologue pins are byte-intact at the shifted address.
- Pins that cannot survive as written: all FNV body hashes (oculus 11, kinematic primary/merge/clear), `kUiPanelSiteBytes` (RIP disps), and call-site pins whose callee moved by a different delta: 0x42B42EA, 0x42B4ED1, 0x43130A5, 0x8D5337 (rel32) and 0x4E70B6 (IAT disp).
- Data: the two EffectsBinary archives are `deflat` (raw deflate) streams holding **only VS/PS** (733 VS, 1995 PS DXBC). `Effects_` (897 blobs) is untouched since Oct 1; `Effects2_` (1831) was rewritten today and the old copy is gone. Of 167 old dumps, 142 are byte-identical in the new archives (hash = EDVR's fnv1a64 over the container, verified on all 167); **25 are in neither archive** (the engine's tone, copy, lighting and projection shaders). They live in the `FREA` asset containers, which are opaque (551 of the 848 files rewritten today); not attempted. Compute shaders are in no archive. Of 475 64-bit constants in `src/`, 328 are present byte-for-byte.
