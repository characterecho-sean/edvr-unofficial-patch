- **What it does.** Docked, with the game's own pad state saying the own pad is in the hangar (r1 1) and the camera in the bay, it seals: outside the held hangar box the producer-B veto (pre-forward), the rig early veto (whole off-screen rigs, the biggest CPU saving), the post-store cull and the sun pair are skipped. Releases: setting off, undock, the camera 2 m out of the box for 10 frames, the own pad leaving the hangar (r1 1 to 0), the pad moving against its shell, a hangar change, a latched bay mechanism record moving against the shell (launch). The rules are `sealStep` in optimized_station_core.h; the Trigger section below has them.
- **Threading (no lock on any per-record path).** Store, producer and rig callbacks run on the render thread and about seven job threads. Each store callback is one Writer on a 3-pass ring keyed by the frame stamp (PassGather); the tick closes a pass only when every writer in it has left, and PassGate counts only a complete pass with a store callback as evidence. An empty or unfinished pass leaves the pad, the camera (CamHold), the latch and shell-motion samples and the rig publication as they were. SeenWindow keeps pads and explicit parts for two informative passes. The snapshot the callbacks read is pinned by a per-thread hazard cell (SnapshotRing::pinThread); every per-call counter is thread-local and flushed once per pass. The rig verdicts are staged in a spare set and committed in one store; a rig the publication does not hold asks the tick to register it (a lookup miss), and the run of vetoes carries across republications. The node cache (class, flags, name per node and lifecycle generation) is a probed table, one name read per node per generation. Lifecycle generation moves at undock, hangar change, ship change and a setting toggle.
- **Sampling.** The pads, explicit parts, mechanism and hangar names are gathered from their nodes' flags on every pass; the near list, the own candidate and the ship tally on every 4th stamp sealed, every 8th unsealed, every stamp with an engage pending. The camera rule does not read these gathers (the camera comes from the pass's context). A ship swap is seen after 10 readings: about 40 ticks sealed (about 0.45 s), about 80 unsealed (about 0.9 s).
- **Latch.** Every latched bay record is measured in the hangar shell's frame (centre and two axes along the shell's own axes, the shell read in the same call, on an informative pass only), so an origin rebase (50.85 m on the on-foot to cockpit transition) is not motion; a backstop re-bases when the shell and every record move by one vector. **The mechanism release is armed only after every latched record has been still for 60 informative passes (about 1 s) after the seal** (a planetary port reports r1 1 while its gate ramps are still closing, flight 1718577b); the sealed poses for the re-seal are taken when it arms, one line says so, and until then mechanism motion neither releases nor counts. Every other release works from the first frame.
- **Logs (30 s, written only for a window that was docked or sealed).** `last N s: sealed for X of Y frames; ... faults (by reason)`, `passes`, `cost` (calls per frame, ms per frame, rig lookups, node names read), `tables` (only when a probe bound was hit), the pad-state probe's summary (only when the game called a docking reader). Event lines: seal, release (with the reason), the waiting line (a stable change only), latch, origin shift, hangar note, sun on and off, on and off. The pad-state probe is the trigger's input and stays; its change lines are the own pad's only, capped at 200 a session.

Arguments: rcx the cluster context, rdx, r8, r9 pointers (r9 the view block: the four inverse-projection rows at +0x1B0..+0x1F0, the depth range floats at +0x234 and +0x238), then two 32-bit stack arguments, the target width and height (5th and 6th; nothing beyond is read). **The game already memoizes it:** this+0x00..+0x77 holds the last build's key (four rows, two floats at +0x40 and +0x44, six 64-bit values at +0x48..+0x70 for the grid, the slice count, the size and the tile) and a call whose inputs compare equal (cmpeqps on the rows, ucomiss on the floats, cmp on the qwords, and a global byte at +0x599C1A4 clear) jumps to the epilogue at 0x58BDD7. A call whose key differs rebuilds both tables and stores the new key at 0x58BD6B. The tables are vectors of 32-byte elements (size at +0x90 and +0xB0, data at +0x98 and +0xB8: the cluster AABBs and the depth-slice AABBs). So the hot loop runs only when the key changed since the LAST call, and in VR two eyes alternating through one slot, or a jittered projection, would defeat it. The detour is at the ENTRY (prologue pinned: 48 8B C4 55 53 56 57 41 54 41 56 41 57 48 8D A8; identity-gated like the store hook; the five moved bytes are mov rax,rsp and two pushes), forwards the six arguments unchanged, and reads only the key block and the tables under __try before and after. ## What ships

## Trigger (v14.23): the game's pad state

The seal trigger is the game's own pad state, read by the pad-state probe (`pad_state_probe.cpp`, `pad_state_core.h`). The own pad is the object the docking reader r0 was last called on in this dock (`GameTrack`); it is reset at undock and at a hangar change, and a switch to another object is logged once. "In the hangar" is the own pad's r1 equal to 1, read fresh (within 1 s). A seal needs: in the hangar, the setting on, Status docked, a valid pad pick and cell, and the camera in the bay for 10 frames. It is immediate: no stillness clock, no load-in or arrival rule, no descent observation, no fallback.

Releases: setting off, undocked, the camera out for 10 frames, the mechanism moved (the latch), the pad moved (ShellMotion), the hangar changed, and the own pad leaving the hangar (r1 1 to 0). After a mechanism, pad-moved or left-hangar release, the next seal needs an r1 0 then 1 edge. A camera or setting release needs none.

Sun: off while the own pad's r0 is 1 (moving in) or 3 (the descent), read fresh. Back on when the pad is out of the hangar, r0 is neither 1 nor 3, and the pad is raised (the geometric height test), or on undock, setting off, or a hangar change. The outpost surface (r0 0) never turns it off.

Lines: the waiting line is `optimized station: waiting (docked=yes, own pad @0abc40, r0=1 r1=1, in hangar=yes, camera=yes, cell=built)`, or `... game pad state unknown (no r0 on this dock) ...`. The sun line is `... sun light inside the hangar off (<why>; r0=1 r1=1; Status docked=yes)`. The probe's own-pad line at a seal or release is `pad state at seal: own pad obj @... r0=.. r1=.. r2=.. r3=.. byte=.. occ=.. o2c=.. o2f=..`, or `own pad unknown`.

Kept for the trigger: the latch (the mechanism release), `ShellMotion` (the pad-moved release), the pick (`pickOwnPad`, `pickOwnPadSticky`), the shell pick (`pickShell`), the held-cell rules (`keepNamedCell`, `heldCellDrops`, `ownSphereKeeps`, `inBayVolume`, `boxCoordsOf`), and the pad-frame release (`padMovedAway`, `localizeCell`, `worldizeCell`). Removed in v14.23: the geometric seal state (`SealState` load-in, moved and still fields), the bay-still and 30 s fallback, `DockWatch` (arrival and descent), `PadStillWatch` (turn), the pad-track instrument, and `ShellHeightSources`.

Core (pure logic, `src/d3d11/optimized_station_core.h`): the trigger (`sealStep`, `sunStep`, `GameTrack`, `gameWaitingLine`), the own-pad pick with the near-tie rule and the sticky state (`pickOwnPad`, `pickOwnPadSticky`), the shell pick (`pickShell`), the held-cell rules (`keepNamedCell`, `heldCellDrops`, `ownSphereKeeps`, `inBayVolume`, `boxCoordsOf`), and the pad-frame release (`padMovedAway`, `localizeCell`, `worldizeCell`).

Engine (`src/d3d11/optimized_station.cpp`): the store judgement, the rig and producer vetoes, the held-cell refresh each frame, the seal tick, and the two status kinds (one line per state change, a 30 s summary line).

Hooks: `store_hook.cpp` (path A, store observer), `kinematic_eval_hook.cpp` (producer-B veto on the engine-motion bracket of 0x4312E00; rig early veto on `rigEvalObserved` emulating Elite's off-screen branch), `vscreen.cpp` (sun-pass skip while sealed), `journal_watch.cpp` (Flags bit 0 as docked), `view_source.cpp` (explorer branch removed).
