## The surfaces

**The table.** The census (2026-09-23, 35 flight logs since 2026-08, every
census frame with GUI draws -- vs `666EF0C4..` vector, `1012E00B..` text,
`A3E5D3FC..` icon -- into an offscreen target, grouped by ratio to the frame's
eye viewport): five groups seen at two or more eye widths more than 1% apart,
agreeing within 10/10000, all format 27, all three families in each:

| ratio (W x H) | evidence |
|---|---|
| 0.2092 x 0.3175 | 539x807 @2576x2544, 589x883 @2818x2784, 1135x1701 @5424x5356; fss_res.h's 908x1361 @4340x4284, 1363x2042 @6510x6426 |
| 0.4187 x 0.2649 | 1078x674 @2576x2544, 2271x1419 @5424x5356 |
| 0.4498 x 0.3726 | 1267x1036 @2818x2784, 2440x1996 @5424x5356 |
| 0.6280 x 0.1367 | 1769x380 @2818x2784, 3407x732 @5424x5356 |
| 0.6783 x 0.1362 | 1354x290 @1996x2121, 1400x300 @2064x2208 |

Left out: powers of two, slivers, eye-sized and larger-than-eye targets, and
one 666x998 surface that kept its pixel size across a 7.5% resolution change.

**Learning.** A render or depth create of an interface surface's shape
(smaller than the eye, no power of two, 16+ px) that matches nothing is
remembered with the internal size it was made against. When ui_depth's
classifier later sees GUI draws land in a surface of that size, its ratio
(against THAT size) is learned, saved to `ui_quality_ratios.txt` beside the
logs, and resized from the next create. Needs `fix.temporal_aa` (the
classifier runs under it); the census needs nothing.

**The internal resolution.** The runtime's recommendation for the frame
(native temporal's `beginFrame`: max over eyes of the game-facing size --
`fix.openxr_resolution`, the FOV trim and the cull guard included), before the
first frame the runtime's swapchain sizing made even (`game_render_size.h`),
times the `.fxcfg`'s HMDRenderTargetMultiplier, truncated: 3070x3032 x 0.65 =
1995x1970, x 0.5 = 1535x1516; the flight's FOV-trimmed 2458x2824 x 0.65 =
1597x1835. Cross-check: the size the game submits (`eyeTextureSize`); a
disagreement is logged (four at most) and a create matches against either.
Last fallback: vScreen's measurement (`vScreenInternalResolution`).

**The match** (inside CreateTexture2D, the game's streaming threads, one
lock): factor = target / HMD Quality (nothing within 1%, capped at 4), the
texture grows by it, `fss_res`'s ring tracks it, its viewports and scissors
are rescaled at the draw-time backstop, copies touching it are counted. Once
per size a session the line names the ratio, the basis and the game's
allocating RVAs (the route to sizing it in the engine, as `vscreen_res.h`
does the on-foot screen).

**Blends** (colour kept, alpha = transmittance T, cleared 1): opaque -> ONE,
ZERO / ZERO,ZERO; over, premultiplied over -> ZERO,INV_SRC_ALPHA; additive ->
ZERO,ONE; **multiply** (ZERO,SRC_COLOR or DEST_COLOR,ZERO) -> the colour
equation into the layer, T untouched, and the same draw once more into a
per-channel transmittance M (cleared 1). Composite: `out = L + F * T * M`.
Exact for any order of the accepted shapes (the rig's CPU model, 1000
sequences; on WARP, two multiplies among five blends, within 3/255).

**Depth and stencil.** A WRITE (the menu panel: ALWAYS / REPLACE, ref 4, write
0x04): the colour goes to the layer, then the draw is issued once more with no
colour target and the game's own depth target and state, so the write lands
exactly as before (rig: bit-identical). A TEST: the layer's depth-stencil
target (the game's format, the layer's size) is seeded from a copy of the
game's with `ui_deferred_depth.h`'s Seeder, each layer pixel reading the game
pixel `floor(p * game/layer + jitter)` -- the redirected viewport's map
inverted -- and the draw tests against it. Seeded at the frame's first tested
draw, again after a game clear or game draw writes the buffer, or when a draw
reads stencil bits not yet copied. Nearest-sample: exact on pixel-aligned
edges (rig: 1.0 under three jitters, 1.25), up to half a layer pixel off
elsewhere. Refused and named: a read-only view, MSAA, an array, another size,
D16; a write or a multiply through the curved screen's own geometry (its
substitution cannot be issued twice); a multiply that also writes.

**The replay's own draws** (a post-tone copy or terminal-canvas tail
`ui_deferred.cpp` captured) are left to the replay: taken twice they would be
drawn twice.

## advanced.ui_replay

`1` (the default, as before): under dlss/dlaa/fsr the deferred UI replay
redraws the holo panels, flight HUD and target sprite after the upscale at the
output size. `0`: it does not; they stay in the reconstructed picture with the
UI depth and the reactive mask. Live; logged as `Deferred UI: advanced.
ui_replay = N`. The A/B for the cockpit HUD.

## Under EDHM

3Dmigoto replaces Elite's shaders, and EDVR hashes what reaches the device:
the replay and the layer, keyed by shader hash (the families above, the
replay's post-tone shaders), stand down under replaced shaders -- the draw
goes into the picture as it always did, intact. The surfaces match by ratio
at the create and do not care (learning does: it keys on the GUI vertex
shaders). This work adds no capture of its own; the replay's 2026-09-16
capture-mask fix (`2c6e820`, `docs/edhm-black-cockpit-2026-09-15.md`) is
untouched and must hold.

## Limits

- What the game draws into an eye AFTER a taken draw lands under it (counted,
  named: "after the UI").
- A sub-pixel viewport origin can cost the layer's last row or column on some
  jitter phases (under the lens mask).
- The 1.25 downsample filters encoded values, the space the game blends in.
- An sRGB door frame or eye view is refused (never measured).
- A refusal with UI in the layer, or a swapped eye, loses one frame's UI in
  one eye, then the layer stands down.
- The on-foot gate lags (about a second): the first on-foot frames after a
  disembark go to the layer, behind the loading screen. With the journal
  watcher off it never holds, and on foot the world goes to the layer again.
