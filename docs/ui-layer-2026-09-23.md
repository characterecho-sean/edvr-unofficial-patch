## The surfaces

**The rule.** The 129 September logs with a frustum on record: each panel's
first sighting (the depth probe names its depth partner), paired with the
render state it was made in -- the render size and the frustum the game is
handed (`native benchmark workload ... game FOV radians`; the legacy VR log's
`GetProjectionRaw`). A panel is, on both axes, a constant times
U = W / 2 tan(vFOV/2), vFOV = atan(tanUp) + atan(tanDown). One row per
distinct state, worst residual over the panels made in it:

| frustum up/down, render | vFOV | old rule (w/W, h/H) | U rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pimax untrimmed ±1.2648, 14 sizes 1995x1970 .. 3901x3226 | 103.3 | ≤ 0.22%, but 19.5% at 2708x2240 and 3901x3226 | ≤ 0.18% |
| Pimax trim 7/5/2 ±1.1778, 1597x1835 (Sean, 5 logs) | 99.3 | 7.85% | 0.20% |
| horizontal trims only ±1.2648, 5 states 1660x1969 .. 2554x3030 | 103.3 | 16.8-26.8% | ≤ 0.28% |
| symmetric and cull-guard frusta ±1.0600, ±0.9657, ±0.8831, 6 states | 82.9-93.3 | 19.3-87.0% | ≤ 0.16% |
| Quest 3 0.9657/1.4281, 5 states 1996x2121 .. 2863x2448 | 99.0 | 8.0-24.9% | ≤ 0.16% |
| Quest 3 trim 2/2/7 0.7536/1.1106, 2307x1652 and 3017x2160 | 85.0 | 90.4-90.8% | ≤ 0.19% |

33 states, 126 log-states (123 logs): every panel within 0.20% there, median
0.04%. Six log-states do not fit: in five the panel was made before a state
change and first seen after it (each fits the earlier state), the sixth is a
2/3-eye buffer, not a panel. `game FOV radians` is the frustum native
temporal's `beginFrame` carries, which the match reads. The constants, in
units of U:

| panel | w x h / U | e.g. Sean trimmed at 0.65 (U 677.9) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5291 x 0.7929 | 358x537 |
| 2a | 1.0576 x 0.6608 | 717x448 |
| 2b | 1.0919 x 0.6822 | 740x462 |
| 3 | 1.1371 x 0.9303 | 771x630 |
| 4 | 1.5885 x 0.3408 | 1076x231 (the first build's fifth seed, 1354x290 on the Quest, is this) |

A match is within 0.5% on both axes (the worst residual 0.28%; 2a and 2b, the
nearest pair, 3.2% apart). At a fixed frustum U is linear in W, so the factor
target / HMD Quality makes a panel the rule's size at the target.

**Learning: two questions, both needed.** IS IT UI -- ui_depth's classifier
saw GUI draws land in a surface whose create matched nothing -- and DOES IT
SCALE, in units of U. A GUI-drawn surface is first *pending*, with its render
size and frustum; the same ratio of U at a U more than 1% different
*learns* it (the menu's 1566x880 at U 788.7 and the trimmed 1346x757 at U
677.9 share a ratio of U -- though flight 4 saw the menu's own surface become
1254x705 at the trim, so the two may be different surfaces); the same pixel
size at such a U
marks it *fixed*, never resized. All three are kept in
`ui_quality_panels.txt` beside the logs (named in the ini; the first builds'
`ui_quality_ratios.txt` held ratios of the render size and is not read).
Needs `fix.temporal_aa` (the classifier runs under it); the census needs
nothing.

**The internal resolution.** The runtime's recommendation for the frame (native
temporal's `beginFrame`, max over eyes; lock-free), before the first frame the
runtime's swapchain sizing made even, times HMD Quality, truncated: 3070x3032 x
0.65 = 1995x1970, x 0.5 = 1535x1516; the flight's FOV-trimmed 2458x2824 x 0.65
= 1597x1835. HMD Quality is the newest `.fxcfg`'s HMDRenderTargetMultiplier,
read on configure and every 5 s on a thread-pool thread, never on the render
thread's frame path or inside a create. Cross-check: the size the game submits;
a disagreement is logged and a create matches against either. Through a
cull-guard or FOV-trim adoption the frame's recommendation is the previous ask
while the game already re-makes its surfaces for the new one (flight 4's
1254x705, P3-1): the host hands native temporal what the game is told
(`askedWidth`), and a create is judged against that first. Last fallback:
vScreen's measurement. The vertical field of view is the frame's own frustum
(native temporal's `beginFrame`, eye 0, lock-free, whatever `fix.temporal_aa`
says); before the first frame it is not known, and nothing is matched (said
once). So the surfaces need EDVR's OpenXR runtime: on the Oculus SDK path no
panel is made bigger.

**The match** (inside CreateTexture2D, the game's creates only): factor =
target / HMD Quality (nothing within 1%, capped at 4); `fss_res`'s ring tracks
the texture, the draw-time backstop rescales its viewports and scissors,
copies touching it are counted. A create of a size decided in the last 2 s
gets that decision again, so a colour target and its depth partner stay one
size though the basis moves between them. Once per size a session a `made
bigger` line names the size, the new size, the factor, the panel ratio, U,
the render size and vFOV, and the game's allocating RVAs.

**If the rule never fires**, the 30 s line says so: `N render and depth creates
examined (M the shape of a surface but on no ratio)` with `none made bigger
yet`, the basis it judged by (`internal WxH ..., with a vertical FOV of D
degrees (U = ...)`), and each GUI-drawn surface it missed is named once as
`pending -- ... with 2 tan(vFOV/2) T (ratio R x R of U = X)` -- the numbers
to compare with the table above. No frustum yet: `the game's vertical field
of view is not known yet`. The match never asked: `0 render and depth creates
examined`, or `no surface created since the key went on`.

**2026-09-23, the engine-side panel sizing -- BUILT, held unmerged until a
flight reads the chains above as `rtt`** (`ui_panel_scale.*`; the bytes and
the arithmetic in `ui_sizing_math.h`; the trace's sections 3 and 5). The
formula's divisions are inlined twice -- the init FUN_144570500 at 0x45706FA,
the view-change recompute FUN_144571180 at 0x4571205 -- as `divss xmm6,
[rip+disp32]` against the game's 1080.0 (0x4DDE660) and 1920.0 (0x4DDE664).
The four disp32s (0x4570707, 0x4570714, 0x4571212, 0x457121F) now point at
two EDVR floats in a read-only page within rel32 of the image, 1080 x f and
1920 x f:

    f = (W_ui x k) / (W_out x k_out) / T, clamped to [1/4, 1]

W_ui x k is the game's own c (the ask x HMD Quality, and k of the frustum it
is told); W_out the runtime's untrimmed recommendation and k_out the true
frustum's k (the native channel now carries the headset's tangents beside
the told ones); T the key's 1.0 or 1.25. Every render-to-texture panel comes
out at stage x W_out x k_out x T / 1920 -- its untrimmed size at HMD Quality
= T, whatever the trim -- and the game makes, lays out, viewports and
depth-partners it itself. At 0.65 (the rig): untrimmed x1.539 at 1.0 and
x1.924 at 1.25; trimmed 7/5/2 x1.790 and x2.238 (the menu's 16:9 screen
3013x1695); capped at x4; x1 when HMD Quality is at or above T.

Safety: the PE stamp and size, both sites' 30 bytes, both prologues and the
three constants must be build 332841's, or nothing is written and `ui
quality: panels: STANDING DOWN, nothing written -- <the failed check> (the
panel formula's shape occurs N time(s) in the game's code)` -- 2 in 332841,
the next build's first clue -- and the matcher sizes the panels it knows, as
before. The operands are swapped once, at the first configure with the key
on, all four or none; after that only the floats change (aligned data
stores), when the factor's inputs have held a new value 10 frames and moved
it over 0.1% -- the recompute runs twice per view change, and a float changed
between the two would part a viewport from its target. Key off: 1080 and
1920 again, bit for bit. Unload: the original disp32s written back; the
float page is never freed. While the engine sizes, the matcher stands aside
(it would size a panel twice; the 30 s line counts `left to the engine's own
panel sizing`) and the sizing chain's implied stage is multiplied back by f.

Lines: once `ui quality: panels: the panel formula's four divisions (init
0x4570707 and 0x4570714, view change 0x4571212 and 0x457121F) now read
EDVR's floats at <page> ...`; per write `ui quality: panels: the engine now
sizes every render-to-texture panel x2.2378 (... read 1080 -> 482.61, 1920
-> 857.97): f 0.4469 = (W_ui 1597 x k 0.8432) / (W_out 3070 x k_out 0.7853)
/ target 1.25 -- HMD Quality 0.65, the game told 2458x2824, its vertical FOV
99.3 degrees, the headset's 103.3. From the next panel init or view
change.`; every 30 s `the engine sizes panels xF since frame N (...)`,
`patched, not sizing -- <why>` or `standing down -- <why>`.

Limits: a panel keeps its size until its next init or view change; the
game's k lags one reconfigure (1254x705 above) while f uses the frame's, so
a panel re-made in that frame is off by the lag's ratio until its next view
change; the inputs come over the native temporal channel -- until all are
known the 30 s line says `patched, not sizing` and the matcher works, and a
later gap (a channel reset) keeps the last factor written.

**The cursor window** (FUN_14453EF80, panel vtbl +0x198, read for this
entry) is the one other reader of a panel's (w, h). It divides the panel's
own +0x3B8/+0x3C0 -- written by FUN_144571180 from these very divisions --
by the UI screen's width and height (the renderer's screen record +8/+0xC,
which the patch leaves alone), normalizes that window keeping its aspect,
pans it with the cursor and hands the panel the cursor's position inside it.
So it is NOT consistent by construction: under the patch a mouse-driven
panel's window grows by 1/f, up to the whole screen, and the cursor crosses
the panel more slowly for the same mouse travel. Focus-driven panels (the
cockpit's) do not use it. The flight that merges this moves the mouse in the
main menu and station services, key on and off; a slow cursor is answered by
scaling that division too (a fifth site, not built), not by keeping the
panels small.

settled state, the chains' surfaces at stage x W_out x k_out x T / 1920 with

**Blends** (colour kept, alpha = transmittance T, cleared 1): opaque -> ONE,
ZERO / ZERO,ZERO; over, premultiplied over -> ZERO,INV_SRC_ALPHA; additive ->
ZERO,ONE; **multiply** (ZERO,SRC_COLOR or DEST_COLOR,ZERO) -> the colour
equation into the layer, T untouched, and the same draw once more into a
per-channel transmittance M (cleared 1). Composite: `out = L + F * T * M`,
exact for any order (the rig's CPU model; on WARP within 3/255).

**Depth and stencil.** A WRITE (the menu panel: ALWAYS / REPLACE, ref 4, write
0x04): colour to the layer, then the draw once more with no colour target into
the game's own depth target and state (rig: bit-identical). A TEST: the
layer's depth-stencil target, seeded from a copy of the game's by
`ui_deferred_depth.h`'s Seeder (warmed with the layer's shaders) through the
redirected viewport's map inverted, at the frame's first tested draw, again
after a game clear or write, or for stencil bits not yet copied; bound only at
the layer's own size. A stencil test against a view with no stencil plane
(D32_FLOAT) is no test (D3D11 passes it): nothing seeded, said once.
Nearest-sample: exact on pixel-aligned edges, up to half a layer pixel off
elsewhere. Refused and named: read-only, MSAA, array, other-size or D16
targets; a write or a multiply through the curved screen's geometry; a
multiply that writes. The replay's own captures are left to the replay.

**The world-screen gate** (the architecture review's P1). The 2D screen stays
in the picture while EITHER the journal says on foot (Status.json Flags2 bit
0, about a second late, 3 s hold through an unknown) OR its own depth is busy:
the most draws a depth target of the screen's exact size (`vScreenPanelSize`)
took in the last frame (`depthProbeDrawsAtSize`), over 64 for 2 frames running
holds it, under 32 for 90 frames running lets go (a frame between restarts the
run). The census behind the numbers, every September log of both installs: on
foot 175 to 17,799 draws a frame (206 samples, 57 sessions; the six of
2026-09-23 381 to 15,612); outside an on-foot session no census shows a
screen-sized depth target with even 10 -- the UI's own take 3 or 4. So a map,
or any 3D scene drawn through the 2D screen, is now left in the picture when
busy, for the same reason as the world; only static screens (the menus,
station services' panels) go to the layer, and an unknown journal with a
quiet screen (the main menu) is still taken. No log caught station services or
a map on the screen: the 30 s line now names the screen's depth by GuiFocus.
The count needs the depth probe, which runs whenever fix.temporal_aa does --
the only time the layer is armed.

## Cost

Per eye: the layer 37.2 MB at 3070x3032 and 1.0 (58.2 MB at 1.25; 74.4 /
116.2 MB at 4340x4284); the composite output, the frame's size; M, the layer's
size once a multiply is seen; once a tested draw is seen, the depth-stencil
target (8 B a pixel for D32S8: 74.5 / 116.4 MB on 3070x3032, 232 MB at 1.25 on
4340x4284) plus a copy of the game's (31 MB at 1995x1970); all released when
not live. The surfaces cost what they would at the target HMD Quality.

**Measured, every 30 s** (the review's "Findings and costs"): the whole route
the layer adds, timed with GpuTimer (no Flush, no wait) in a FIFO read oldest
first and summed per eye-frame -- the layer's clear, the depth-stencil seed
(the copy plus the Seeder's passes, P3-4), the multiply (its clear and the
draw's second issue), each write-back's re-issue, the composite -- as median/p95
ms per eye-frame the stage ran in, and the route's own per-eye-frame sum. The
UI draws themselves, moved into the layer, are the game's work and untimed. An
eye-frame with an interval that had no free timer, did not measure or came
late is left out, and counted. The memory line names what is allocated right
now, by eye, with its size -- the estimate above sums resources that exist
only once a multiply, a tested draw or a copy-refusing frame is seen.

## Limits

- What the game draws into an eye AFTER a taken draw lands under it (counted,
  named: "after the UI").
- A sub-pixel viewport origin can cost the layer's last row or column on some
  jitter phases (under the lens mask).
- The 1.25 downsample filters encoded values, the space the game blends in.
- An sRGB door frame or eye view is refused (never measured).
- A refusal with UI in the layer, or a swapped eye, loses one frame's UI in
  one eye, then the layer stands down.
- The world-screen gate's depth signal holds two frames after the world
  starts (those two go to the layer, behind the transition); the journal's
  about a second. A 3D screen drawing 64 or fewer depth-bound draws a frame
  would be taken (none seen: 175 is the on-foot minimum).
