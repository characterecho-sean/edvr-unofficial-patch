**2026-09-23, the engine-side panel sizing -- BUILT, held unmerged until a
flight reads the chains above as `rtt`** (`ui_panel_scale.*`; the bytes and
the arithmetic in `ui_sizing_math.h`; the trace's sections 3 and 5). The
formula's divisions are inlined twice -- the init FUN_144570500 at 0x45706FA,
the view-change recompute FUN_144571180 at 0x4571205 -- as `divss xmm6,
[rip+disp32]` against the game's 1080.0 (0x4DDE660) and 1920.0 (0x4DDE664).
The four disp32s (0x4570707, 0x4570714, 0x4571212, 0x457121F) now point at
two EDVR floats in a read-only page within rel32 of the image, 1080 x f and
1920 x f:

    f = (W_ui x k) / (W_out x k_out) / T, clamped to [1/4, 1]

W_ui x k is the game's own c (the ask x HMD Quality, and k of the frustum it
is told); W_out the runtime's untrimmed recommendation and k_out the true
frustum's k (the native channel now carries the headset's tangents beside
the told ones); T the key's 100 or 125, as 1.0 or 1.25. Every
render-to-texture panel comes out at stage x W_out x k_out x T / 1920 --
its untrimmed size at HMD Quality = T, whatever the trim -- and the game
makes, lays out, viewports and depth-partners it itself. At 0.65 (the rig):
untrimmed x1.539 at 100 and x1.924 at 125; trimmed 7/5/2 x1.790 and x2.238
(the menu's 16:9 screen 3013x1695); capped at x4; x1 when HMD Quality is at
or above T.

Safety: the PE stamp and size, both sites' 30 bytes, both prologues and the
three constants must be build 332841's, or nothing is written and `ui
quality: panels: STANDING DOWN, nothing written -- <the failed check> (the
panel formula's shape occurs N time(s) in the game's code)` -- 2 in 332841,
the next build's first clue -- and the panels stay at the game's own size
until the patch is re-keyed. The operands are swapped once, at the first
configure with the key on, all four or none; after that only the floats
change (aligned data stores), when the factor's inputs have held a new value
10 frames and moved it over 0.1% -- the recompute runs twice per view
change, and a float changed between the two would part a viewport from its
target. Key off: 1080 and 1920 again, bit for bit. Unload: the original
disp32s written back; the float page is never freed. While the engine
sizes, the sizing chain's implied stage is multiplied back by f.

Lines: once `ui quality: panels: the panel formula's four divisions (init
0x4570707 and 0x4570714, view change 0x4571212 and 0x457121F) now read
EDVR's floats at <page> ...`; per write `ui quality: panels: the engine now
sizes every render-to-texture panel x2.2378 (... read 1080 -> 482.61, 1920
-> 857.97): f 0.4469 = (W_ui 1597 x k 0.8432) / (W_out 3070 x k_out 0.7853)
/ target 125% -- HMD Quality 0.65, the game told 2458x2824, its vertical FOV
99.3 degrees, the headset's 103.3. From the next panel init or view
change.`; every 30 s `the engine sizes panels xF since frame N (...)`,
`patched, not sizing -- <why>` or `standing down -- <why>`.

Limits: a panel keeps its size until its next init or view change; the
game's k lags one reconfigure (1254x705 above) while f uses the frame's, so
a panel re-made in that frame is off by the lag's ratio until its next view
change; the inputs come over the native temporal channel -- until all are
known the 30 s line says `patched, not sizing` and the panels are the
game's own size, and a later gap (a channel reset) keeps the last factor
written.

**The cursor window** (FUN_14453EF80, panel vtbl +0x198, read for this
entry) is the one other reader of a panel's (w, h). It divides the panel's
own +0x3B8/+0x3C0 -- written by FUN_144571180 from these very divisions --

panel's window grows by 1/f, up to the whole screen, and the cursor crosses
the panel more slowly for the same mouse travel. Focus-driven panels (the
cockpit's) do not use it. The flight that merges this moves the mouse in the

**Blends** (colour kept, alpha = transmittance T, cleared 1): opaque -> ONE,
ZERO / ZERO,ZERO; over, premultiplied over -> ZERO,INV_SRC_ALPHA; additive ->
ZERO,ONE; **multiply** (ZERO,SRC_COLOR or DEST_COLOR,ZERO) -> the colour
equation into the layer, T untouched, and the same draw once more into a
per-channel transmittance M (cleared 1). Composite: `out = L + F * T * M`,
exact for any order (the rig's CPU model; on WARP within 3/255).

**Depth and stencil.** A WRITE (the menu panel: ALWAYS / REPLACE, ref 4, write
0x04): colour to the layer, then the draw once more with no colour target into
the game's own depth target and state (rig: bit-identical). A TEST: the
layer's depth-stencil target, seeded from a copy of the game's by
`ui_layer_seed.h`'s Seeder (warmed with the layer's shaders) through the
redirected viewport's map inverted, at the frame's first tested draw, again
after a game clear or write, or for stencil bits not yet copied; bound only at
the layer's own size. A stencil test against a view with no stencil plane
(D32_FLOAT) is no test (D3D11 passes it): nothing seeded, said once.
Nearest-sample: exact on pixel-aligned edges, up to half a layer pixel off
elsewhere. Refused and named: read-only, MSAA, array, other-size or D16
targets; a write or a multiply through the curved screen's geometry; a
multiply that writes.

the most draws a depth target of the screen's exact size (`vScreenPanelSize`)

eye-frame with an interval that had no free timer, did not measure or came

## Limits

- What the game draws into an eye AFTER a taken draw lands under it (counted,
  named: "after the UI").
- The same inversion inside the HDR phase has no counterpart: a draw the layer
  does not take, issued in the HDR target after a taken HUD draw (the frosted
  label quads vs `2CECEC30..`, the canopy, other families), was over that HUD
  draw in stock and is under it now, because the HUD layer is composited over
  the whole frame. Not seen to matter yet; `tools/ui_quality_test` pins it.
- A sub-pixel viewport origin can cost the layer's last row or column on some
  jitter phases (under the lens mask).
- At 125 the downsample filters encoded values, the space the game blends in.
- An sRGB door frame or eye view is refused (never measured).
- A refusal with UI in the layer, or a swapped eye, loses one frame's UI in
  one eye, then the layer stands down.
- The world-screen gate's depth signal holds two frames after the world
  starts (those two go to the layer, behind the transition); the journal's
  about a second. A 3D screen drawing 64 or fewer depth-bound draws a frame
  would be taken (none seen: 175 is the on-foot minimum).
- A pixel shader that reads SV_Position and is taken into the layer sees the
  layer's pixels, not the game's. Three programs are remapped by exact bytes
  (`ui_holo_remap.h`: the two hologram sphere programs and the frosted base).
  Of the 13 layered pixel shaders audited from the station censuses, no
  other reads it; the maps, the galaxy map and the on-foot menus are not
  censused.
  A new layered shader is checked with `python tools\dxbc_disasm.py` on its
  `edvr_logs\shaders` dump: an `SV_POSITION` row with `xy` used in the input
  signature.
